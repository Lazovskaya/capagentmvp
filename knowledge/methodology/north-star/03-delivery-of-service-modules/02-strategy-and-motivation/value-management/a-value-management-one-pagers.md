## Value Management– Define the Value Case for your Transformation

**Module Overview**

Translate customer value to concrete design principles for the project team and measurable outcomes for the project. Based on customer input, design the value case, map it to business capabilities and create a design manual for each functional project team

- None

**Prerequisites**

- Define and track business value based on Design decisions
- Align of customer requirements to Industry Market Standards in a specific industry
- Help eliminate ‘Regret Spend’ through slippage, added cycles, and project redo’s.
- Focus on end to end business value and its realization throughout the program

**Benefits**

- Strategy map with strategic objectives and KPI’s
- Mapping of strategic objectives to business capability scope
- Complete set of value drivers contributing to strategic objectives
- Design manual for each functional team with relevant design principles
- KPI definition with timelines, resources and responsibilities
- Value Governance structure

**Deliverables**

- Business Capability

**Successor Modules/ Next Steps**
