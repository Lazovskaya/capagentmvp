**Module Overview**

**Main Steps**

A **business capability model** depicts what a business does to reach its strategic objectives (its capabilities), rather than how it does it (its business processes). Business capabilities are the connection between the business strategy and business execution.

A **business capability heatmap** is a method for classifying business capabilities on selected context. Context can be used to describe business scope, capability emphasis *(innovation, risk, development areas)* etc.

## Module Scoping and effort Estimation

- Does the customer require to do business capability mapping to identify the priorities required by the target operating model and hence drive architecture?

- What is the LOB scope for the business capability mapping?

- Which customer business divisions/units are in scope for capability mapping.

- Are business capabilities harmonized across business division or they differ significantly.

- What should be the depth of business capability module deliverables– Informal, Light, Core

  - Informal : 2 hrs. of enablement session
  - Light :  2 strategic LOBs
  - Core: All Customer LOBs

- Available customer  documents – Strategy Map, Business capability mapping, Existing IT landscape .
