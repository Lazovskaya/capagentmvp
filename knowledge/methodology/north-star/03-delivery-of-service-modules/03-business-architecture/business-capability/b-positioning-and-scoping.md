## Business Capability Mapping

A **business capability heatmap** is a method for classifying business capabilities on selected context. Context can be used to describe business scope, capability emphasis *(innovation, risk, development areas)* etc.

**Benefits:**

- Gain visibility on the as-is and to-be operating model of an organization.
- Align Business and IT priorities - Ensures the business-critical priorities are addressed in the IT Architecture and roadmap design.
- Identify the potential areas of investment with maximum impact on strategic goals. .

## Artifact – Business Footprint Diagram

A **Business Footprint Diagram** is a visual tool that integrates strategy, business, solution, and technology architecture domains by aligning strategic objectives with the supporting business and IT operating model. It typically shows organizational units, goals, capabilities, processes, solutions, technologies, and locations—grouped into layers—to provide a comprehensive view of the architecture scope. This helps assess current and future states, supporting strategic planning and transformation initiatives.

**Benefits:**

Business Footprint Diagram

**Business Footprint Diagram**

- Strategic Alignment – It links business goals with IT and solution components, ensuring architectural decisions support strategic priorities.
- Holistic Visibility – It provides a comprehensive view across business, solution, and technology domains, helping identify overlaps, gaps, and dependencies.

## Artifact – Product Map

A Product Map is a visualization within the SAP EA Methodology capability view, which captures recommended Products or Solution Components per Business Domain or Business Area with the Solution Capabilities that are covered by those  components.

.

**Benefits:**

- Helps to describe the functional positioning of SAP Solution Components and Products, that support designated Business Domains or Business Areas and should be enabled to meet specific business requirements or objectives
- Delivers a Bill of Material, which supports a proposed target solution architecture
- Can be used to describe an industry-specific Product or Solution Component offering
