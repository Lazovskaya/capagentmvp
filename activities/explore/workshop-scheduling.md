## Workshop scheduling & agendas

Turn a completed `conversion-scoping` business process scope into a ready-to-send, complexity-
ordered workshop schedule and per-session Fit-to-Standard agendas — framed as confirmation/delta-
resolution, not from-scratch process design, since most of a Brownfield conversion's process
already exists and works. Surfaces open ADR questions per Business Area and keeps technical-blocker
items out of the business-discussion agenda.
