## Architecture decision tracking

Maintain one cumulative Architecture Decision Record log across the whole engagement — raised
into by any phase's skill/agent the moment a real, gating decision is identified (Instance
Strategy, a CVI/licensing gate, a `not-Clean-Core` extensibility tier, a downtime-tier choice), and
updated whenever new evidence (a call transcript, workshop notes, a presentation) resolves or adds
to an open decision. Always available as its own artifact — never reconstructed once at final SAD
drafting, by which point context and rationale have faded.

**Cross-cutting, not phase-bound in practice:** listed under Discover because a real decision can
surface as early as the first Readiness Check triage, but this activity runs alongside every other
phase's activities for the life of the engagement, not once and done.

**Owner:** Architect
**Engagement(s):** Greenfield / Brownfield conversion / Build and Extend / AMS & Run
**Status:** In progress
