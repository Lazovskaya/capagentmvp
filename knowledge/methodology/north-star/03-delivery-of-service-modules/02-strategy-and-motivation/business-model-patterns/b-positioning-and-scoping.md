## Module Introduction

The Business Model Canvas provides a clear, visual framework to understand and design how an organization creates, delivers, and captures value—forming a strategic foundation for enterprise architecture planning. Business model patterns help identify recurring, proven approaches that align business goals with architectural decisions, accelerating innovation and reducing complexity in transformation initiatives.

## Motivation and Benefits of the Business Model Canvas and Patterns

- Strategic Alignment: Ensures architecture planning is directly linked to business goals and value creation.
- Accelerated Innovation: Speeds up ideation and transformation by applying proven business model patterns.
- Impact Transparency: Clarifies how changes affect business capabilities, enabling informed decision-making.

**Benefits**

**Understand current business models**

Gather the value creation, value delivery and value capture perspective of current business models

**Assess Impact**\
Translate business model changes into insights by identifying affected capabilities.

**Apply Business Model Patterns**\
Leverage proven patterns to explore strategic changes and new value creation opportunities.

## Artifact – Business Model Canvas

The **Business Model Canvas** is a visual framework that outlines the key elements of how an organization creates, delivers, and captures value. Its nine building blocks cover everything from customers and value propositions to operations, partnerships, and finances. In the context of Enterprise Architecture, it provides a strategic anchor that connects business goals with architectural decisions, ensuring alignment across all layers of transformation.

**Benefits:**

Business Model Canvas

A

- Provides a comprehensive overview of the business model, fostering shared understanding across stakeholders.
- Links business strategy with architecture planning to guide targeted and value-driven transformations.
- Identifies opportunities and challenges early, enabling informed, architecture-aligned decision-making.

## Artifact – Business Model Patterns

**Business Model Patterns** are proven, repeatable approaches to designing value creation, delivery, and capture—based on the 55+ patterns such as “freemium,” “subscription,” or “long tail” models. The Business Model Canvas is systematically assessed to identify which building blocks require changes to align with the chosen pattern's value creation approach. In Enterprise Architecture, these patterns support connecting business model shifts to required differentiating business capabilities.

**Benefits:**

Business Model Patterns

A

- Provides a guided approach to rethinking the business model using proven concepts.
- Accelerates the identification of viable changes by focusing on pattern-driven adjustments.
- Helps identify and prioritize business capabilities that create competitive advantage and require targeted investment.

## Module Scoping

Understand the relevant Business Divisions that are in scope

- a. Is the strategy defined on group/corporate level?
- b. Are there divisional or regional specific strategies?
- c. Is there a difference between group strategy and divisional/regional strategies?
- d. How are the interlinkages between the different strategies in the group?

The main effort driver is the number of Business Divisions that are in scope.(BD).
