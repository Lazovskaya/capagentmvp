## Module Delivery

**Main Steps**

**Artifact Overview**

A **business capability heatmap** is a method for classifying business capabilities on selected context. Context can be used to describe business scope, capability emphasis *(innovation, risk, development areas)* etc.

- Strategy Map ( Mandatory)

**Prerequisites**

- Gain visibility on the as-is and to-be operating model of an organization.
- Align Business and IT priorities - Ensures the business critical priorities are addressed in the IT Architecture and roadmap design.
- Identify the potential areas of investment with maximum impact on strategic goals.

**Benefits/Outcomes**

- Business Process Scoping and Mapping (Optional)
- Application architecture
- Instance strategy
- Roadmap
- Transition strategy

**Successor Artifacts**

**Identify** business capabilities areas under customer industry domain. Use Level 1-3 from LoB, Business Area to Business Capability.

**1**

With Customer agree on  criteria for **classification** – classification such as:

- In-scope/out-of-scope
- Pain point/opportunity
- Priority
- fulfillment

Etc.

**2**

**Classify** business capabilities according to the defined classification criteria to build the business capability heatmap.

**3**

Create different **views** of the business capability model based on:

- As-is state
- Target state
- Transition state
- Business unit/division

Etc.

**4**

- Product Map Generator( Tool)
- LeanIX ( Tool)
- SAP Reference Business Architecture( Reference Content )

## Artefact Creation: Business capability Map – Prepare

**Prepare:**

- Review the Customer Strategy Map or the strategy map generated in Strategy module.
- Check if customer has business capability map. Decide with customer if the customer’s map should be used (depending on quality, completeness etc.). Otherwise use the industry business capability map from RBA content in PMG (preferred).
- Based on the customer  industry download the  business capabilities from the SAP Product Map generator and add the classification attributes agreed with the customer. Use [template](https://sap.sharepoint.com/:x:/r/teams/TransformationHubCoreConfidential/Shared%20Documents/General/02_Initiatives/Modularization/EA%20Domain%20and%20Modules/02%20-%20Business%20Architecture/Module%20Delivery%20Suitcase/Business%20Capability%20%20data%20collection%20template.xlsx?d=w5f645f83be774a89b77b15d529d364e4&csf=1&web=1&e=wlj42x) and share it with customer to collect additional information.

Note- If working with Customer Business capabilities map then PMG

download is not required.

- Based on our experience, questionnaires sent out to customer before the workshop to collect information did not work well. Therefore, we typically rely on the other sources mentioned and the interviews.
- For LeanIX, follow the steps as mentioned in [Reference Business Architecture ](https://docs-eam.leanix.net/docs/reference-business-architecture)

| --- | --- | --- |
| Service Scoping = Understand scope of capability mapping exercise | In-scope | In-scope of architecture work |
|  | Out-of-scope | Out-of-scope for service, i.e. capability will not be discussed as part of architecture work |
|  |  |  |
| Service Delivery = Actual capability heatmapping | As-Is | Capability available in current business architecture |
|  | To-Be | Capability part of future business architecture |
|  | To-be-decided | Not yet clear if capability is needed |
|  |  |  |
|  | Pain Point | Capability is a pain point today (e.g. capability is not available, capability is available but related processes inefficient etc.) |
|  | Priority | Priority of capability for business is low-medium-high-critical |
|  | Opportunity | Opportunity areas. |
|  | Fulfillment | Currently the fulfillment of the capability is – High, medium, Low |
|  | Business Unit/ division | Capability is relevant for business unit A,B and C but not D. |
|  | Business Value/importance | Base/Competitive/Differentiator |
|  | Level of Customization | Low, Medium, High |

**Main Steps**

**Artifact Overview**

A **business capability assessment** analyzes the identified business capabilities from the business capability heatmap in terms of certain criteria in more detail. The assessment allows to prioritize those capabilities with highest strategic impact that can be leveraged as an input for the target architecture and roadmap design.

- Strategy Map ( Mandatory)

**Prerequisites**

**Benefits/Outcomes**

- Business Process Scoping and Mapping (Optional)
- Target Application Architecture
- Roadmap
- Instance strategy
- Transition strategy

**Successor Artifacts**

Decide on **level of assessment**. The assessment could be done on L2 or L3 level of the business capability heatmap

**1**

Select **criteria** for assessment such as:

- Business benefits
- Business readiness
- Other dimensions (e.g. geographical impact)

Etc.

**2**

**Assess** business capabilities according to the defined criteria.

**3**

**Prioritize** business capabilities according to the defined criteria

**4**
