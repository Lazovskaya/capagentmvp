## Business Data Objects: Getting started with the basics

The RBA/RSA 2504 release covers a first wave of **SAP Business Data Objects** for cross-industry processes. Goal is to cover a first set of key Business Data Objects and build the respective relationship to the SAP Business Capability Model (BCM). Moving forward, more Business Data Objects will be included in the next releases.

**Business Data Objects** (BDO’s) are **structured representations of business entities** \
(like customers, orders, or invoices) used **within SAP systems** to **model, store, and manage business data:**

- Represent business entities or concepts (e.g. Sales Order)
- Group together related data fields and business logic
- Are used to support data integration, reporting, transactions, and process automation.

## Objective of BDO in SAP Reference Architecture

With the latest addition of SAP Business Data Cloud to the portfolio of **strategically important** applications , a clear **focus on business data**, including business data objects, becomes clear. SAP BDC is a unified data foundation that allows organizations to access, integrate, model, and analyze business data across SAP and non-SAP systems, while preserving its business meaning and context. This strategic focus is also reflected in the SAP Reference Architecture

BDO’s provided in SAP Reference Architecture are **closely aligned with SAP’s data offerings** and provide the required context. This helps Enterprise Architects to help overcome application specific names and segmentation challenges

Business Data Objects in SAP Reference Architecture provide a **consistent taxonomy** across processes to define data distribution. Having a consistent taxonomy available allows for a shared language between **business and IT** and to bridge transactional and analytical worlds.

## Moving forward with BDO’s in SAP Reference Architecture

A **larger scope of BDO’s** in the SAP Reference Architecture, and making it available to broader audiences in the respective **Enterprise Architecture Tools** paves the way for the following benefits:

- BDO’s in SAP Reference Architecture help SAP customers, architects and partners to get a **deeper understanding of Data Architecture**
- BDO’s show how data is modeled, managed, and consumed across different systems and provide the respective **business context**.
- Business Data Objects ensure data is understood and organized in terms of what it means to the business and how it is **used in business processes**.

- SAP LeanIX users working with SAP Reference Architecture will be able to easily build and **extend** their **inventory of Data Objects** mapped to the respective SAP Applications.
- Example: by mapping the relationship of a Business Data Object to a Business Capability and the Application Factsheet, users can easily learn which application uses which data.
- Additionally, Business Data Objects create the **foundation for** users to create diagrams visualizing the **flow of data** between applications effectively.

- Once made available in SAP LeanIX, users will be able to select an industry and identify **industry-specific architectures** for Public Cloud and Hybrid deployments, **including the respective Business Data Objects**. This allows for an even clearer view on Data Architectures, taking into account the various industry-specifics.
