Components

Factory component map

Anything here could in principle be authored by SAP, skills.cloud.sap is a shelf SAP owns, with a third-party lane, and the boundary only moves one way. So each component declares whether we author it or pull it in.

Fill is posture, blue we author, green we pull in. Border is readiness, none not started, dotted in progress, solid ready. Combine any filters; select none to see everything. Click a component for what it is and what its posture means in the repo. Selecting activities above filters this map to their components.

In the repo, adopt means no authored files: npx skills add ... --copy vendors someone else's skill in and factory-lock.json records where from. Own means authored in the factory repo, and deleting .claude/skills/ must leave the expertise intact in knowledge/.

Strategy

Tool strategy

native the tool reads our file as-is; the adapter only places it   adapted content must be rewritten into the tool's format   absent no mechanism; handled elsewhere   ? unassessed
Only skills, agents and gates vary. Artifacts and Knowledge need nothing from any tool, and they are the two we own.
