## Initial Recommendations : Executive Perspective

**Motivation Domain**

- Analyze and assign the strategic priorities and goals to business domains
- Assign pain points to business domains and prioritize
- Identify processes per business domain that may benefit from innovation, explore available innovation with OEMs, carry out techno-commercial feasibility analysis and fix the innovation items’ scope

**Data/ Information Domain**

- Assign pain points and innovation items to business domain/ value flow scope
- Identify components of the envisaged solution, the key data flows between the different components and capabilities that each component will enable, and capture them in the form of a solution diagram

**Process Domain**

- Select artefacts that’ll prove transformation success, formulate an acceptance process and identify roles
- Select commercial model and OLAs for ERP cloud adoption
- Assess deployment of domain specific solutions for business continuity with ERP moving to cloud
- Discuss business critical customization with OEM and decide on back to standard/ clean core compliant customization

**Network/ Location Domain**

- Finalize the business unit scope, i.e. plants and warehouses

**Timeline Domain**

- Revalidate internally estimated timelines with SIs and have it extended to an effort estimate

**People Domain**

- Predict behaviour based on the role of each stakeholder, classify them suitably and formulate an appropriate strategy to mange them
- List down the key transformation activities/ artefacts, frame a basic RACI, identify leads for EA, design and program governance and detail out the RACI with what each form of governance needs to do for each of the artefacts
