## Template Assessment & Guidelines

**An SAP template** is a definition of how business processes and data models will be configured in SAP to support a specified business scenario or a specific operating model.

The purpose of this module is to **assess/design the Global Template strategy** for a customer by determining the needs and benefits that will drive such a strategy and determining the key building blocks that can be used to operationalize the strategy.

**Benefits:**

- Derivation of the SAP Template strategy linked to strategic goals and related mandates
- Determination of Template drivers, definition and governance
- High level planning of template build and rollout phases
- Assessment of Template impact based on deployment options and target architecture
