## Requirements intake

Normalize a free-form ask, Jira/ALM ticket, email/workshop notes pack, or greenfield idea into one
`requirements.md` with explicit Source, Goal, acceptance criteria only when present in the source,
and Open questions for gaps. Stops silent invention of AC before fit-to-standard or solution-design
Mode B. Does not create backlinked backlog items — that remains `backlog-drafting` after the FTS/
design chain exists.

**Owner:** Functional
**Engagement(s):** Greenfield / Brownfield conversion / Build and Extend / AMS & Run
**Status:** In progress
