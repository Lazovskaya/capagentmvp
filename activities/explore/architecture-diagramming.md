## Architecture diagramming

Create, merge, or adapt SAP architecture diagrams in native `.drawio` format, grounded in SAP's
official Reference Architecture catalog (~30 architectures, ~144 diagrams), with SAP's real color
palette, icon style, and layout conventions. A shared, cross-phase supporting skill — feeds SAD
drafting's System Architecture section and any activity that needs an SAP-native-looking landscape
or integration diagram, including custom diagrams of non-SAP systems integrated with SAP.

**Owner:** Architect
**Engagement(s):** Greenfield / Brownfield conversion / Build and Extend
**Status:** In progress
