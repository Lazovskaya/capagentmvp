## Defect pattern analysis

Periodically (sprint / month / hypercare wrap-up), look backwards across a period of resolved
defects and ask why they keep happening: cluster by cause, rank by business weight, propose a
permanent fix on the eliminate/prevent/detect/absorb ladder. Distinct from the per-ticket triage
flow — this is the Prevent stage, run on a schedule, not reactively.
