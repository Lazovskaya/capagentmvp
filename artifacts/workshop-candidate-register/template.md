## Review checklist

- [ ] Every candidate cites one or more stable transcript segment IDs.
- [ ] Consolidated candidates retain every original segment ID.
- [ ] Unclear statements are recorded as open questions, not inferred requirements or decisions.
- [ ] No candidate is represented as an approved FTS decision, design record or Jira work item.
