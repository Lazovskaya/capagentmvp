## Conversion scoping

Turn a Readiness Check's Simplification Item findings into a scoped business-process list — marked
Changed/Unchanged/Out of scope — grounded in SAP's official Reference Business Architecture
(RBA/RSA) process catalog, with Unchanged processes explicitly enumerated (not summarized) and a
complexity estimate per Changed process. Cross-references the client's Application Map when
available. Any Business-Decision-flagged row is raised into the ADR log immediately.

**Owner:** Architect
**Engagement(s):** Brownfield conversion
**Status:** In progress
