## Business Capability Mapping

A **business capability model** depicts what a business does to reach its strategic objectives (its capabilities), rather than how it does it (its business processes). Business capabilities are the connection between the business strategy and business execution.

A **business capability heatmap** is a method for classifying business capabilities on selected context. Context can be used to describe business scope, capability emphasis (innovation, risk, development areas) etc.

**Benefits:**

- Gain visibility on the as-is and to-be operating model of an organization.
- Align Business and IT priorities - Ensures the business-critical priorities are addressed in the IT Architecture and roadmap design.
- Identify the potential areas of investment with maximum impact on strategic goals.
