## Delivery Guide

## Business Process – Agenda Flow Overview

**Validate Business Process Variant**

**Mapping As-Is and To-Be application**

**Business Process Heatmapping**

**Derive business capability and product map**

Review each in-scope business process variant from the SAP Reference Business Architecture.

Map the as-is and potential to-be applications to the business capabilities in the business process variant

Heatmap on business activity level according to defined criteria like pain point/opportunity, standardization potential, or strategic importance.

Review final results of the derived business value flow and solution value flow diagrams and heatmaps.

**Review results**

Derive a Business Capability Map and a Product Map, as based on SAP reference content, business and solution value flows are directly linked to business and solution capabilities.

## Module Delivery Steps

Input from scoping: list of all in-scope business process variants from SAP RBA. Validate jointly with the customer for each business process variant:

1. Guide the customer through the embedded business process modules, segments, and business activities, and business capabilities.
2. Adjust the business process variant according to customer specific requirements by adding or removing modules, segments, activities, and capabilities. (This should be done carefully as no process blueprinting is done in this module, and SAP RBA should be leading).

3. Suggested attributes:

   - **Pain Point:**

     - Yes
     - No

   - **Strategic Importance:**

     - Commodity: Business capability maintains commodity and operational needs but does not provide any competitive advantage.
     - Differentiation: Business capability is important to achieve excellence in order to compete with and differentiate from others.
     - Innovation: Business capability is critical to completive advantage.

   - **Standardization Potential:**

     - High: Capability is well-aligned with SAP standards and can be implemented with little to no customization.
     - Medium: Some variations exist, but most of the capability can be standardized with moderate effort.
     - Low: Capability is highly customized or fragmented, with limited potential for standardization.

   - **Innovation Potential**

     - High:  There is clear potential to significantly improve or reimagine processes using modern SAP innovations (e.g., SAP BTP, AI, ML, RPA, embedded analytics)
     - Medium: Some potential exists to introduce modern technologies to improve efficiency or flexibility.
     - Low: Little to no benefit expected from applying new technologies. The processes are either already optimized, standardized, or not suitable for technology-driven innovation at this time.

4. Demonstrate to customer how SAP advises to address pain points, innovations, and standardization potential. Document the results as recommendations.

5. Discuss major architecture decisions to be taken
