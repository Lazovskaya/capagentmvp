## Agent-readiness assessment

Score the client's processes for whether they should be handed to agents: volume, exception rate, how reversible the decisions are, and whether the data an agent would need is actually reachable. Output names both the candidate processes and the ones to leave alone. This is the bridge from delivery work into SAP's Autonomous Enterprise, and it is a consulting deliverable, not a product.
