## Fit-to-standard workshops

Run each in-scope process against SAP standard, decide standard-versus-delta per step, and get it signed. Produces the process bucket and a sign-off record naming who accepted what. The discipline is recording the delta and its justification together, a delta with no reason becomes a modification nobody can defend at the next upgrade.
