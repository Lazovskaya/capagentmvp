## The Transformation Readiness Assessment FAQs

**Transformation Readiness Assessment FAQs**

**What is the value of this assessment?**

Customer  Obtains an understanding of how well they have conceived, defined and planned their transformation program and what they need to do more

SAP  Gets an insight into the customer’s intent and mindset towards cloud, clean core, tool chain, and AI/ innovation, for proper engagement planning and product/ services positioning, to ultimately facilitate adoption

**-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**How long does it take to complete the assessment?**

End to End  1 – 2 days (8 – 16 hours)

Response fill-up  2 – 3 hours/ perspective & maximum 8 hours for all 3

Analysis & recommendation  1 – 2 hours/ perspective & maximum 4 hours for all 3

Customer review & adjustment  1 – 2 hours/ perspective & maximum 4 hours for all 3

**----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**Which customer stakeholders are required to participate in the assessment?**

Enterprise Architect(s)  If there’s a formal EA Practice and/ or an EA

Functional Leads  If EA like skills are there in business functions, but no EA Practice or EAs

IT Leads  If there are neither any EAs, nor any EA like skills in business functions

**Which customer segments does this assessment apply to?**

Customers who intend to/ or have embarked on adopting SAP

S/4HANA greenfield or brownfield or pilot or roll outs etc.

S/4HANA footprint extension with e.g. TM/ EWM, post RISE migration via lift & shift

LoB/ platform/ data/ AI solutions e.g. Ariba OR BTP/ BDC OR Joule

**-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**Are there any additional prerequisites or preconditions required to conduct the assessment?**

Transformation stage-based selection of applicable perspectives  discovery – only executive perspective; ideation – executive & business; planning – executive, business & solution

**----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**What is the outcome of the assessment?**

An initial measure of readiness against each applicable perspective

An initial set of recommendations in line with the initial readiness level

Adjusted readiness level and recommendations after customer review

## The Transformation Readiness Framework

- To be adequately prepared for any business and technology transformation, an organization needs to be ready with its enterprise architecture primarily, and additionally, in other fields like value, process and program management as well
- To accelerate realization of transformation benefits, SAP recommends a few differentiators/ benefit multipliers based on technology trends and best practices, e.g. cloud, clean core and tool chain adoption, which are also important considerations for preparedness
- As part of the SAP North Star methodology, therefore, the transformation readiness framework has been evolved with artefacts contained in the SAP enterprise architecture framework, value, process & program management and the SAP recommended differentiators

## Determining the Assessment Approach

| Approach Determinant | Customer Situation | Assessment Approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transformation Stage | Customer is trying to identify what the organization wishes to achieve through the transformation, i.e. the intent | Assess only the Executive Perspective readiness |
|  | Customer is trying to define business concepts, like, value drivers, capabilities, processes etc. that can satisfy the intent | Assess both Executive and Business Perspective readiness |
|  | Customer is trying to determine the solutions and system models that can enable the business concepts | Assess readiness of all three Perspectives, i.e. Executive, Business and Solution |
|  | Customer is in the middle of transformation execution or has just gone live or is in steady state with no future IT transformation envisaged | This assessment is not applicable in such situations |
| Variance in Readiness Levels of Different Business Domains or Business Divisions or Business Units or Regions | More or less uniform level of readiness across all business domains, divisions and regions | One assessment by a central team representing the entire organization |
|  | One or two stand out business domains, divisions and regions, but rest have a uniform level of readiness | Separate assessments by the stand-out parts/ teams and one by a central team representing rest of the organization |
|  | Significant differences in opinion on readiness levels among different business domains, divisions and regions | Different assessments by different parts of the organization/ teams have to be carried out |
| EA Practice Structure and Skill Set & Level avalaible in the Organization | There’s no defined EA practice and EA like skills are mostly concentrated in the IT and project management teams | Only suitably skilled people from IT and project management teams should provide responses |
|  | There’s no formal EA practice, but EA like roles and people with EA like skills are embedded in business | Suitably skilled people from different business organizations should form a team that provides the responses |
|  | There’s a formal EA practice or at least an individual EA with visibility across business domains/ divisions/ units and regions | Whether single or multiple assessments should be done, is to be decided by the EA practice and all responses should be routed through them |

**Decide on the Assessment Approach**

## The Transformation Readiness Assessment : Scoping & Positioning

**UNDERSTANDING THE CUSTOMER SITUATION**

- **Business Landscape Situation :**

  - Customer is a vertically integrated Energy major, with significant presence in traditional sources and a growing interest in renewable sources
  - They’re in upstream (exploration & production), downstream (petro retail & petrochemicals) and renewables (wind & wave energy) businesses
  - Upstream & downstream are well established businesses spread across globally, while the renewables business is nascent and Europe focussed

- **IT Landscape Situation :**

  - The customer’s core has three distinct sets of SAP ECC landscapes for the three business sectors, split across regions in most cases
  - Upstream has a new S/4HANA global template on RISE, which is live in Malaysia, in execution in UAE and with roll outs planned in other locations
  - Downstream had several business-critical customizations on ECC, which have been lifted & shifted to S/4HANA on RISE and just gone live
  - Renewables is on a central ECC server, uses only FI and MM and is being brainstormed for a transformation to S/4HANA on RISE
  - The customer’s ECC landscape and transformation situation to S/4HANA is depicted graphically in the next slide

- **Team Composition Situation :**

  - Historically, the customer’s IT decisions have been driven by the CIO with the help of a few key functional & technical solution experts
  - With frequently changing IT trends, since the past one year, an Enterprise Architect (EA) has been recruited to manage overall architecture
  - There’s no formal architecture practice yet, the EA reports into the CIO and has the solution experts reporting into him through a dotted line

## The Transformation Readiness Assessment : Scoping & Positioning

**DETERMINING THE ASSESSMENT APPROACH**

- **Picking the applicable Perspective(s) :**

  - The upstream business is in the midst of execution and is hence excluded
  - The downstream business has just concluded a go live and is hence excluded
  - The renewables business is considering a transformation and is hence suitable for the assessment
  - Since they’re in the transformation conception stage, only the Executive perspective is applicable

- **Determining the # Assessments Needed :**

  - The renewables business is nascent and only Europe focused – hence only one assessment is deemed sufficient

- **Identifying Suitable Customer Stakeholders :**

  - Since the customer has an EA in their set up for the last one year, the assessment can be run primarily with the customer EA
  - However, if there’s need for functional/ technical expertise during the conversation, the customer EA can involve such experts from their side
