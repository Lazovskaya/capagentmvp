## SAP Value Assurance – Planning the Digital Transformation Service Component: Analytics Strategy

**Analytics and Data Management is a key component of the Intelligent Enterprise and the SAP Business Technology Platform. With the Analytics Strategy service component, customers get guidance for their road to the Intelligent Enterprise from analytics and data management perspective focusing on target architecture options, components and best practice concepts.** \
**It includes topics like SAP S/4HANA embedded analytics, SAP data warehousing, analytic frontends and integration of data lakes. It covers on premise as well as cloud solutions. The workshop is structured into several building blocks and can be adjusted to the needs of the customer.**

**Business needs**

- Modernize analytical capabilities by introducing intelligent technologies to enable business users to be more efficient, agile, and real-time
- Utilizing full capabilities of SAP Business Technology Platform (considering components like SAP S/4HANA, BW/4HANA, SAP Data Warehouse Cloud, SAP HANA Cloud, SAP Data Intelligence and SAP Analytics Cloud)
- Get guidance for the latest SAP Analytics solutions, Data Warehousing and planning components, as well as integration with data lakes and other SAP or Non-SAP analytical applications on premise or in the cloud.
- Maximize the value of existing SAP investments while minimizing the risk of deployment
- Integration of public cloud solutions like SAP S/4HANA Cloud, SAP SuccessFactors, SAP Concur, SAP Sales Cloud, SAP Commerce Cloud, etc.) into a holistic analytics concept

**Delivery approach and scope**

- Scope and prepare the customer workshop by using a reference capability map to understand customer’s situation and needs

- Service execution is on-site or remote following three main sections:

  - Identify required capabilities, main pain points and challenges
  - Product mapping & knowledge transfer (e.g. SAP S/4HANA Embedded Analytics, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP BW/4HANA, \
    SAP Data Warehouse Cloud and SAP Data Intelligence)
  - Target analytics solution landscape and a high level mile stone plan

**Value and benefits**

- Guidance for the customer to define a future-proven analytics and data management architecture based on business and IT requirements with a strong focus on the SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) to build a foundation for the Intelligent Enterprise
- Customer-specific roadmap with a high level mile stone planning
- Define target analytics solution landscape (options) for the next 3 to 5 years (cloud or on-prem)
