- Includes modules of all Enterprise Architecture domains to address each customer individually.

- **Similarities**

- General Service flow: positioning  scoping  effort and team  agenda  delivery  result presentation

- The architecture “Vision” is an initial sketch on what the overall outcome of the architecture work is expected to be. To ensure success, relevant stakeholders should be identified and the context from both business and IT should be gathered. This includes already formulated business and IT strategies, which will be refined for the selected scope within the strategy &motivation domain. Finally the “statement of architecture work” defines the scope / requirements of what to do in the specific domains.

- The Strategy / Motivation domain is the first part of “business architecture” deriving objectives (value drivers) from company strategic priorities and long-term goals establishing company's vision. This domain ensures that the enterprise architecture is not only aligned with the business strategy but also motivates stakeholders by clearly communicating the benefits and rationale behind architectural decisions and transformations.

**E2E Business Process**

A structured representation of value generation for an enterprise that creates a defined outcome for a stakeholder. We differentiate between Generic Business Processes (template) and Business Process Variants (implementable process).

**Business Process Module**

A business process which typically consists of one or multiple lower or same level granularity business processes (segments or nested modules).

**Business Process Segment**

A business process consisting of one or multiple Business Activities which are grouped to form a small, re-usable process.

**Business Activity**

A granular business process that creates a defined value for an internal or external stakeholder.

**Enterprise Domain**

Basic structure of an Enterprise according to it’s core functions.

**Business Domain** (BD)

Level 1 grouping of Business Capabilities  according to functional similarities.

**Business Area** (BA)

Level 2 grouping of Business Capabilities along functional similarities.

**Business Capability** (BC)

They define “what” the business does, constituting “a particular ability or capacity that a business may possess or exchange to achieve a specific purpose or outcome” .

**Solution Capability** (SC)

*Solution Capabilities are implementations of Business Capabilities, realized by a combination of software products (one or many).*

**Solution Component**

A solution component represents a software product (app or service) or a group of software products with a unique identifier (PPMS ID)

**Solution Process Flow**

A solution process is represented by typically multiple solution process flows. An overview flow should show how the different sub-flows integrate to implement the entire process.

**Solution Activity** (SA)

Solution Activities represent an action which takes place in a solution component. A business activity is typically implemented via a set of solution activities within one or across different solution components.

**Solution Process**

A solution process is an implementation of a business process (or a subset of it) via a defined set of solution components.

## SAP Reference Architecture – Metamodel

> A **set of eight Generic E2E Business Processes** describe and provide the foundation and building blocks for standardized business process modelling. The top level entity starts with E2E Business Process.
>
> The **Business Capability Model (aka One BCM)** has been defined in close collaboration with Product and Solution Management across all Line of Business (LOB’s).  The top level entity starts with Business Domain.
>
> **How** the stakeholder creates value and achieves an outcome by performing a series of steps using information and applications, and how technology supports it.
>
> **What** the organization does to generate value, independent of how technology supports it.
>
> A **Solution Capability** or **Solution Component Map** depicts all Solution Capabilities and Solution Components that are needed to implement a Business Capability. A Business Capability might be supported by one, none or many Solution Capabilities.
>
> **How** technically IT supports the business to drive value in the organization

> - Architecture transformation services represented – as Lego blocks ( Modules) under EA domain
> - Each Module can be delivered independently (or part of delivery chain ) with reference content, tools and methodology
> - Each Module can be configured as per the customer situation
> - Modules integrate with each other for input /output with predefined parameters.
>
> Our Architecture development methodology is aligned with TOGAF and we have further identified the most commonly used Architectural units under each domain based on our  40 yrs of expertise.
>
> Each module is a kind of lego block for has a well defined purpose.
>
> Modules don’t work in Silos but are integrated  for an end to end  delivery . Exception is in some cases where in module  is equivalent to an delivery – Instance Strategy or Analytical Strategy  etc.
>
> Business Model Pattern- The purpose of this module is identify business model pattern based on customer’s strategy and to identify what capabilities are needed to fulfil the business model pattern.
>
> Example – Manufacturing customer  looking forwad to sell directly to customers via  subscription (Pay per Use ) and  Direct to Consumer ( D2C)
>
> Strategy Mapping & Assessment helps understand organization Strategic Goals, drivers and objectives which are basis for the  required business capability, business processes and the transformation map.
>
> The **business process scoping and mapping** depicts what a business processes are in scope for the architecture design. In addition, based on predefined criteria and optionally by leveraging BPI Process Insights, a process heatmap can be established. (The SAP Reference Business Architecture provides content for end-to-end business processes and process variants.)
>
> Business Capability Scoping and Mapping - A **business capability model** depicts what a business does to reach its strategic objectives (its capabilities), rather than how it does it (its business processes). Business capabilities are the connection between the business strategy and business execution.
>
> A **business capability heatmap** is a method for classifying business capabilities on selected context. Context can be used to describe business scope, capability emphasis *(innovation, risk, development areas)* etc.  Heatmaps are powerful high level communication tool during the architecture definition process and serves multi-purpose usage.
>
> Transition Scenario Evaluation - The purpose of this module is to discuss the key transition scenarios (greenfield, brownfield, Bluefield) towards the target architecture (including consolidation options) and determine the relevant customer consolidation mix options/transition scenarios and evaluate based on a set of criteria, the optimal choices for the customer.
>
> Initiative Catalog & Prioritization - The purpose of this module is to determine the set of transformation initiatives, list and cluster the change events belonging to each initiative, link the overall initiatives (including those already in process/planned by the customer that have an impact on the roadmap) to relevant business objectives and then prioritize them in a value/efforts matrix
>
> Implementation & Rollout strategy -  purpose of this module is to assess/design the implementation and rollout strategy of a given customer roadmap cluster. Asses/design in the first place the right implementation choices and then design/assess the optimal rollout strategy for the customer projec

> - Architecture transformation services represented – as Lego blocks ( Modules) under EA domain
> - Each Module can be delivered independently (or part of delivery chain ) with reference content, tools and methodology
> - Each Module can be configured as per the customer situation
> - Modules integrate with each other for input /output with predefined parameters.
>
> Our Architecture development methodology is aligned with TOGAF and we have further identified the most commonly used Architectural units under each domain based on our  40 yrs of expertise.
>
> Each module is a kind of lego block for has a well defined purpose.
>
> Modules don’t work in Silos but are integrated  for an end to end  delivery . Exception is in some cases where in module  is equivalent to an delivery – Instance Strategy or Analytical Strategy  etc.
>
> Business Model Pattern- The purpose of this module is identify business model pattern based on customer’s strategy and to identify what capabilities are needed to fulfil the business model pattern.
>
> Example – Manufacturing customer  looking forwad to sell directly to customers via  subscription (Pay per Use ) and  Direct to Consumer ( D2C)
>
> Strategy Mapping & Assessment helps understand organization Strategic Goals, drivers and objectives which are basis for the  required business capability, business processes and the transformation map.
>
> The **business process scoping and mapping** depicts what a business processes are in scope for the architecture design. In addition, based on predefined criteria and optionally by leveraging BPI Process Insights, a process heatmap can be established. (The SAP Reference Business Architecture provides content for end-to-end business processes and process variants.)
>
> Business Capability Scoping and Mapping - A **business capability model** depicts what a business does to reach its strategic objectives (its capabilities), rather than how it does it (its business processes). Business capabilities are the connection between the business strategy and business execution.
>
> A **business capability heatmap** is a method for classifying business capabilities on selected context. Context can be used to describe business scope, capability emphasis *(innovation, risk, development areas)* etc.  Heatmaps are powerful high level communication tool during the architecture definition process and serves multi-purpose usage.
>
> Transition Scenario Evaluation - The purpose of this module is to discuss the key transition scenarios (greenfield, brownfield, Bluefield) towards the target architecture (including consolidation options) and determine the relevant customer consolidation mix options/transition scenarios and evaluate based on a set of criteria, the optimal choices for the customer.
>
> Initiative Catalog & Prioritization - The purpose of this module is to determine the set of transformation initiatives, list and cluster the change events belonging to each initiative, link the overall initiatives (including those already in process/planned by the customer that have an impact on the roadmap) to relevant business objectives and then prioritize them in a value/efforts matrix
>
> Implementation & Rollout strategy -  purpose of this module is to assess/design the implementation and rollout strategy of a given customer roadmap cluster. Asses/design in the first place the right implementation choices and then design/assess the optimal rollout strategy for the customer projec
