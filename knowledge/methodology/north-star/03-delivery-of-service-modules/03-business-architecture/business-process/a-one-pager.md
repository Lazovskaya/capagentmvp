A **Business Process** is a combination of value-adding business activities to fulfill a defined business goal and create a valuable result for a stakeholder.

The business process driven approach focuses on the *How* or the different ways in which a business creates value (end to end business value flow variants) and from them, derives what the business should do (business capabilities) to reach its strategic objectives.

The Business Process module can optionally also derive a Business Capability Map and a Product Map, aligning with SAP reference content where business and solution value flows are directly linked to business and solution capabilities.

**Benefits:**

- Gain visibility on the process  capability  application linkage.
- Understand which application enables which process.
- Ensure business critical priorities are addressed in the IT Architecture.
