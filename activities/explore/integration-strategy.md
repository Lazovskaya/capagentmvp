## Integration strategy

Build the program's Integration Strategy document following ISA-M methodology, across four
human-gated phases (Context & Objectives, Hybrid Integration Platform/ISA-M, Mechanisms & Design
Decisions, Governance/Catalogues/Roadmap). A parallel workstream, not a sequential stage — starts
after initial discovery and is refined as Readiness Check and Business Process Scope outputs land,
consuming both directly. Every confirmed Key Design Decision feeds the engagement's ADR log.

middleware per interface class, PI/PO migration approach) is exactly `EPMCSAPAI-283`'s scope
(Integration architecture strategy, one of the nine not-yet-built domains `agents/sad-assembly`
composes) — not yet reconciled; see `skills/integration-strategy/SKILL.md`.
