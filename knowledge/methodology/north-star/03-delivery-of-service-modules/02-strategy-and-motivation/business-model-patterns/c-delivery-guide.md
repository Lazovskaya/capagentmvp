## Delivery Guide

## Module LeanIX Setup

A new factsheet type is required to import the business model pattern library.

## LeanIX Setup – Meta Model – Create New Factsheet Type

1. Go to *Administration*  *Meta Model Configuration*
2. Select *New Fact Sheet Type*
3. Create new fact sheet type Business Model Patterns.
4. Refer to this as reference for configuration: [https://sap-eaf.leanix.net/sapeadeliverytransformationhub/admin/factsheets](https://sap-eaf.leanix.net/sapeadeliverytransformationhub/admin/factsheets)

Business Model Pattern have selected differentiating business capabilities. In addition, an organization can have one or multiple business model patterns. These relationships need to be created.

## LeanIX Setup – Meta Model – Link Business Model Pattern to Organization

1. Go to *Administration*  *Meta Model Configuration*
2. Select Fact Sheet *Organization*
3. Add Business Model Patterns to Demand Management section.
4. Reference: [https://sap-eaf.leanix.net/sapeadeliverytransformationhub/admin/configuration/Organization/fields](https://sap-eaf.leanix.net/sapeadeliverytransformationhub/admin/configuration/Organization/fields)

Business Model Patterns content is currently not yet official SAP reference content. An excel is provided in the module suitcase for upload to LeanIX.

## LeanIX Setup – Fact Sheets– Upload Business Model Patterns

1. Go to *Inventory  Add Fact Sheet Excel Import*
2. Select the excel provided in the module suitcase
3. Upload the patterns to LeanIX.

- **Business Model Pattern Report**

  - report type *Matrix*
  - Base fact sheet type: *business capability*
  - Reference: [https://sap-eaf.leanix.net/sapeadeliverytransformationhub/reports/matrix-businesscapability/96bc3b93-bd1f-4a94-bc89-4f8e9c7bf7c0?returnUrl=%2Freports%2Foverview%2Fcollection%2F11d54fcd-3bae-4b07-9ea5-e7c13fcb8d08](https://sap-eaf.leanix.net/sapeadeliverytransformationhub/reports/matrix-businesscapability/96bc3b93-bd1f-4a94-bc89-4f8e9c7bf7c0?returnUrl=%2Freports%2Foverview%2Fcollection%2F11d54fcd-3bae-4b07-9ea5-e7c13fcb8d08)

## Module Delivery Steps

1. Introduce customers to business model canvas
2. Introduce customer to business model patterns
3. Discuss with customer current business model to get an overview and helps you to draft an initial version of the business model canvas as-is.

## Step 1: Baseline Preparation

**Baseline Preparation**

- **Business Model Canvas**

  - Upload canvas template from module delivery suitcase to LeanIX

## LeanIX Setup – Diagram – Business Model Canvas

## Business Model Canvas As-Is

Based on gathered information as pre-requisites (customer’s vision & strategy, organizational structure, and discussion in baseline preparation workshop), draft an initial version of the Business Model Canvas As-Is

1

1. Discuss with customer the prepared business model canvas as-is.
2. Change aspects in the canvas based on customer’s feedback.
3. Discuss with customer planned changes to the existing business model and document those changes in the canvas already.

## Step 2: Derive Business Model Canvas

**Derive Business Model Canvas**

1. Based on information from previous meeting, already identify as preparation what business model patterns could be relevant to the customer.
2. Present short listed patterns to customer.
3. Agree with customer on one business model pattern that should be analyzed in more detail.
4. Discuss with customer changes to the As-Is business model based on the selected pattern
5. Document these changes in the business model canvas.

## Step 3: Explore Business Model Patterns

**Explore Business Model Patterns**

## Business Model Canvas Impact based on Pattern

Discuss major impacts of the pattern on the existing elements in the canvas.

2

Map key changes and new aspects for the categories in the canvas based on the patterns.

3

1. Prepare a mapping of differentiating business capabilities for the selected pattern.
2. Review with customer in the workshop these capabilities and adjust where necessary.
3. Perform a joint evaluation on current maturity of these business capabilities.

## Step 4: Architecture Impact Mapping

**Architecture Impact Mapping**

## Business Capabilities Mapping

Select from standard RBA content the top differentiating business capabilities for the selected pattern (currently manual task, no reference mapping available).

Hint: you can leverage GenAI as first proposal but needs manual refinement.

4

Add these capabilities to the customer’s organization and classify the business capabilities to the current maturity of the organization.

5

1. Before review workshop, check again the LeanIX reports and necessary adjustments.

2. Optional: create a dashboard in LeanIX for the customer presentation

3. Review with customer the results and make last adjustments to the following produced artifacts:

   - Business Model Canvas As-Is
   - Business Model Canvas Impacted Areas
   - Business Capability Heatmap

4. Sign-off deliverables with customer

## Step 5: Review
