**Purpose of Technology Capability Model**

**WHY**

**Explore how Technology can support the Business:** Enable Customers and Enterprise Architects to navigate through the various capabilities Technology can offer and learn details about these

**Connect the outside-in view with SAP’s offering:** Start with a neutral stance on Technology, regardless of business-context and provider-specifics. Then identify, which specific SAP software/services/solutions are available to enable any of the generic Technology Capabilities

**Capture a customer’s current state:** As a customer or Enterprise Architect, model an enterprise’s current landscape of Technology Capabilities and related SAP Solutions

**Create a Technology transformation road map**: Plan upgrades, migrations or transitions of specific Technology Capabilities and underlying Software/Services towards desired future states
