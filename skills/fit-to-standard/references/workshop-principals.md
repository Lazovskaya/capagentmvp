# Fit-to-Standard Workshop Best Practices

*Source: SAP Activate Methodology Accelerator S4H_885 (May 2023) — General Principals slide*

Use this checklist to verify a workshop is set up and run correctly. Every item applies unless there is an explicit documented reason to deviate.

## Pre-workshop

- [ ] **Plan in detail** with templates for workshop inputs and outputs prepared before the first session.
- [ ] **Set boundaries and understand expectations** at the start of each workshop — roles, decision authority, what is and is not in scope today.
- [ ] **Provide process, value and strategic context** at the start of each workshop so BPEs understand why decisions matter.
- [ ] **Build off the Business Driven Configuration Questionnaire(s)** filled out by the customer — do not start from a blank sheet.

## During workshop

- [ ] **Focus on business value** and tie every decision to the business case and value drivers.
- [ ] **Access SAP Best Practices Explorer** (scope item process diagram, process model, test scripts) to explain and demo S/4HANA functionality — do not rely on slides alone.
- [ ] **Discuss upgrade impacts** — flag any scope that has or may be impacted by a system upgrade; refer to "What's New" Help documentation.
- [ ] **Prioritise scope and configuration** — not everything can be decided in one session; parking lot anything that needs more information.
- [ ] **Document decisions, assumptions and business impacts** of new processes immediately — not at end of day.
- [ ] **Time-box to drive decision making** — FTS is not a training session. Keep momentum.
- [ ] **Table parking-lot items** explicitly rather than leaving them as unresolved open questions.

## Post-workshop

- [ ] **Produce detailed documentation immediately** and align with the customer while decisions are fresh.
- [ ] **Formal sign-off of key decisions and required configuration** before Realize phase starts — no undocumented verbal agreements.

## Hard rule on outputs

Every workshop must produce:
- Business Driven Configuration Questionnaire (Final)
- Backlog (Prioritised)
- Meeting Minutes and Action Items (Reviewed and Finalised)
- Sign-off of Key Decisions
- Updated Integration and Interface List
- Updated Process Flows
- Delta Requirements list
