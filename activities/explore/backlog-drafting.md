## Backlog drafting

Convert deltas and design decisions into sized, owned, traceable work items in the work-intent system, each carrying a link back to the process decision that caused it. Includes an AC quality check and review before Jira/Cloud ALM push. Ungrounded asks (no design-record) are refused and routed via `requirements-intake` / fit-to-standard — not drafted. Traceability is established here or not at all, and defect triage downstream depends on it entirely.

**Owner:** Delivery Manager
**Engagement(s):** Greenfield / Brownfield conversion / Build and Extend
**Status:** In progress
