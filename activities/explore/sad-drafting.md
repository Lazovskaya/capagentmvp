## SAD drafting

Draft or review EPAM's Solution Architecture Document for a Rollout, Conversion
(Greenfield/Bluefield/Brownfield), or generic SAP solution implementation — filling EPAM's real
SAD `.docx` template, engagement-type-aware, grounded in Readiness Check findings and Business
Process Scope where available, with real Instance Strategy, Sizing Strategy, RICEFW/Clean Core,
Data Migration Architecture, and Dual Maintenance/Retrofit decision matrices. Feeds every gating
decision it produces into the ADR log rather than letting it wait for Section 13 to be drafted.

**Relationship to `sad-assembly` note:** this activity's output overlaps with the Realize-phase
`sad-assembly` activity's `technical-sad` composition (both cover landscape/security/integration/
extensibility/data/infrastructure ground) — not yet reconciled; see `skills/sad-drafting/SKILL.md`.
