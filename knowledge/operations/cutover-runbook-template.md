# Operations: cutover runbook template

Use one runbook per release or cutover rehearsal. This is a reusable template,
not production evidence until a named owner validates it against a real run.

## Preconditions

- Scope and go-live decision are approved.
- Backup/restore or rollback path is confirmed.
- Required access, owners and escalation route are tested.
- Monitoring and operational baseline are available.
