## The Transformation Readiness Assessment FAQs

**Transformation Readiness Assessment FAQs**

**What is the value of this assessment?**

Customer  Obtains an understanding of how well they have conceived, defined and planned their transformation program and what they need to do more

SAP  Gets an insight into the customer’s intent and mindset towards cloud, clean core, tool chain, and AI/ innovation, for proper engagement planning and product/ services positioning, to ultimately facilitate adoption

**-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**How long does it take to complete the assessment?**

End to End  1 – 2 days (8 – 16 hours)

Response fill-up  2 – 3 hours/ perspective & maximum 8 hours for all 3

Analysis & recommendation  1 – 2 hours/ perspective & maximum 4 hours for all 3

Customer review & adjustment  1 – 2 hours/ perspective & maximum 4 hours for all 3

**----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**Which customer stakeholders are required to participate in the assessment?**

Enterprise Architect(s)  If there’s a formal EA Practice and/ or an EA

Functional Leads  If EA like skills are there in business functions, but no EA Practice or EAs

IT Leads  If there are neither any EAs, nor any EA like skills in business functions

**Which customer segments does this assessment apply to?**

Customers who intend to/ or have embarked on adopting SAP

S/4HANA greenfield or brownfield or pilot or roll outs etc.

S/4HANA footprint extension with e.g. TM/ EWM, post RISE migration via lift & shift

LoB/ platform/ data/ AI solutions e.g. Ariba OR BTP/ BDC OR Joule

**-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**Are there any additional prerequisites or preconditions required to conduct the assessment?**

Transformation stage-based selection of applicable perspectives  discovery – only executive perspective; ideation – executive & business; planning – executive, business & solution

**----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------**

**What is the outcome of the assessment?**

An initial measure of readiness against each applicable perspective

An initial set of recommendations in line with the initial readiness level

Adjusted readiness level and recommendations after customer review
