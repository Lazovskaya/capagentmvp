## Brownfield conversion pipeline

Entry point for a full SAP Brownfield (system) conversion SDLC — orients the consultant on which
of the five stages (Readiness assessment, Sizing, Scoping & conversion-type, Workshop preparation,
Workshop-to-backlog) applies, states what the next stage needs, and routes to the right stage
skill. Meta for the user, not a client deliverable; produces no gate evidence by itself. Sits one
level below `factory-onboard`, which routes here for full Brownfield-conversion requests.

**Owner:** Delivery Manager
**Engagement(s):** Brownfield conversion
**Status:** In progress
